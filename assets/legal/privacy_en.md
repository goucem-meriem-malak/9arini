# Privacy Policy
Version 1.0 · Effective 20 November 2026
## 1. Who is responsible
9arini ("we") is responsible for your data in the 9arini app.
## 2. Data we collect and why
- Account: email address, name, role (student, parent or tutor) and language. We use it to run your account. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see it.
- Tutor profile: bio, subjects, wilaya and years of experience, shown to signed-in users so they can find you.
- Classes: classes you create or join, join requests, posts, comments, reactions, poll votes and files you upload. We use them to run the classes.
- Children (parents only): first name and school level, stored in your account and used when you request a seat. Only the tutor of the class sees the name you choose to send.
- Reviews: your rating and text, shown with your name.
- Notifications: a device token and a list of notifications, to send you updates.
- Reports and blocks: to keep the community safe.
- We do not collect GPS location. Wilaya and commune are chosen or typed by you.
- This version has no advertising, no analytics and no payments.
## 3. Where it is stored
Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging). Google acts as our processor. Our database is in a European region.
## 4. Who can see what
Tutor profiles and class pages are visible to signed-in users. A class space is visible only to its tutor and approved members. Your name and role are visible to the tutor and members of classes you join.
## 5. Children
A child under 13 can only be used through a parent account. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13.
## 6. Keeping and deleting data
We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting removes your account, profile, memberships, comments, reviews, children and tokens. Reports about content may be kept to protect users. Short-lived technical backups may remain with our providers.
## 7. Your rights
In Settings you can export a copy of your data, change your name and delete your account. You can also contact us. You have the rights given by applicable law, including Algerian law on personal data.
## 8. Security
Access rules are enforced on the server, and connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure.
## 9. Changes
We may update this policy. We ask you to accept new versions.
## 10. Contact
Write to us at the support address shown in Settings, About.
